## Image Guidelines
- **Resolution**: High-definition, at least 800x800 pixels (aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:3 recommended).
- **Subject**: Clear view of leaves, flowers, or diagnostic plant parts in natural daylight.
- **Background**: Natural or neutral background without heavy blur or obstruction.
- **Fallback**: The MediPlant app displays verified botanical fallback images and icons whenever local asset files are being populated.
